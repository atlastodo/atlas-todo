Delete your Atlas Todo account

Deletion has a 30-day grace period: sign in during those 30 days to cancel it.

In the app

  1. Open Atlas Todo on Android, or the web app at app.atlastodo.dev, and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with your password.

Without the app

If you can no longer use the app, email privacy@atlastodo.dev from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete the account.

What is deleted

Your account is scheduled for deletion and permanently removed after 30 days. Sign in during those 30 days to cancel. After that, everything tied to the account is deleted: your email address and login data, sessions, keys, all your encrypted tasks and other content, and your attachments. Projects you shared stay available to their other members. Nothing is kept after the 30 days.

This applies to accounts on app.atlastodo.dev. If your account is on a server someone else runs, ask that server's operator.
