## v0.1.21

### Features
- Open habits from anywhere: press H on the web and desktop, or hold + on a phone (slide up onto the habits button, or let go and tap it)
- Close the habits drawer on a phone by pulling it down, from its header or from the top of its list

### Fixes
- Open a held habit's or card's menu where the finger was
